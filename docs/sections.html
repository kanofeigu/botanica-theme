Sections & theme settings

Botanica ships with 49 sections. Some power a specific template (product, collection, cart, blog…), others can be added to any page in the theme editor, and two — the header and footer groups — appear on every page of your store.

Header & navigation Footer Product Collection Cart Blog & articles Search & system pages Customer account Password page Content & merchandising Theme settings

Section groups: Botanica uses Online Store 2.0 section groups for the parts of your store that appear everywhere. The Header group contains the Announcement bar and Header sections; the Footer group contains the Footer section. Edit them once in the theme editor and they update on every page.

Header & navigation

2 sections
SectionFileWhat it does
Announcement bar announcement-bar.liquid A slim bar above the header for store-wide messages — shipping offers, launches, or promotions. Supports multiple announcements that rotate. Part of the header group, shown on every page.
Header header.liquid Your store's main header: logo, navigation, search, account, and cart. Menus support mega-menu blocks (collection list, promo card, or custom links), and the header can stick to the top of the screen while scrolling.

Theme settings

12 groups

Global settings live under Theme settings in the theme editor and apply across your whole store:

Setting groupWhat it controls
LogoYour store logo, logo width, and favicon.
ColorsColor schemes — background, text, button, and accent colors you can assign to sections throughout the theme.
TypographyHeading and body fonts (Fraunces and Inter by default — choose any font from Shopify's library) plus overall text scale.
LayoutMaximum page width and horizontal/vertical grid spacing.
ButtonsButton style (solid or outline) and corner radius.
InputsCorner radius for form inputs.
Product cardsCard image aspect ratio (1:1, 4:5, or 3:4) and card corner radius.
BadgesToggle sale, sold-out, care-level, and light-requirement badges on product cards.
Social mediaLinks to your Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok, X (Twitter), and YouTube profiles.
Search behaviorPredictive search, and whether results include products, articles, and pages.
CartCart type (drawer or page) and the free-shipping progress bar threshold.
Currency formatShow or hide the currency code next to prices.

Looking for something specific? Check the FAQ for common how-tos, or contact support — we're happy to point you to the right setting.
