Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find yours? Contact support — we reply within two business days.

How do I switch between the Botanica, Home & Decor, and Wellness presets?

Open the theme editor (Online Store → Themes → Customize), click the theme name at the top of the editor sidebar, and choose the preset you want. If you're adding the theme from the Shopify Theme Store, you can also select a preset when you add it to your library.

Switching a preset replaces your current theme settings with that preset's defaults, so we recommend duplicating your theme first (Actions → Duplicate) as a backup.

How do I install the theme?

In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes, then click Add theme → Upload zip file in the theme library and select the Botanica ZIP. Once uploaded, click Customize to start editing, and Actions → Publish when you're ready to make it your live theme. See the installation guide for the full walkthrough.

How do I set up my menus — and the mega menu?

First create your menus under Online Store → Navigation in your Shopify admin. Then, in the theme editor, open the Header section and assign your menu with the Menu setting.

To turn a top-level menu item into a mega menu, add one of the Header section's mega-menu blocks — Mega menu — Collection list, Mega menu — Promo card, or Mega menu — Custom links — and enter the exact name of the menu item it should attach to.

What image sizes do you recommend?
  • Hero & slideshow: at least 2400 × 1200 px, landscape. Keep important content away from the edges so it survives cropping on mobile.
  • Product images: use one consistent ratio across your catalog. The default product-card ratio is 4:5, so 1600 × 2000 px works beautifully. You can change the card ratio under Theme settings → Product cards.
  • Collection images: match your product-card ratio (4:5 by default) for a tidy grid.
  • Logo: SVG or a transparent PNG at 2× the display size. Favicon: 32 × 32 px PNG.

Use JPG for photographs and PNG or SVG for graphics with transparency, and aim to keep each image under roughly 300 KB for fast loading.

How do I enable the cart drawer and quick add?

Go to Theme settings → Cart and set Cart type to Drawer. Shoppers will see their cart slide in from the right without leaving the page; choose Page if you prefer the classic cart page.

Quick add is built into every product card: hovering a card reveals an add-to-cart bar, and adding an item opens the drawer automatically. The drawer also includes a free-shipping progress bar — set your threshold under Theme settings → Cart.

How do I add my logo and favicon?

In the theme editor, open Theme settings → Logo. Upload your logo image, adjust the Logo width slider (50–300 px), and upload a favicon — it appears in browser tabs and bookmarks.

Which languages does the theme support?

Botanica ships with a complete English locale (en.default.json), so every piece of theme text is translatable. To sell in additional languages, add them under Settings → Languages in your Shopify admin and translate the theme with Shopify's free Translate & Adapt app — no theme code changes needed.

Is Botanica fast? Any performance tips?

Yes — Botanica is built performance-first: a single lightweight stylesheet, minimal JavaScript, responsive images, and lazy loading throughout. To keep your store fast:

  • Compress images before uploading and keep them near the recommended sizes.
  • Limit the number of installed apps, and remove ones you no longer use.
  • Prefer image sections over autoplaying video where possible.
Which browsers are supported?

Botanica supports the latest two versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge, plus current mobile browsers on iOS and Android. Older browsers (such as Internet Explorer) are not supported.

How do I change colors and fonts?

Open Theme settings → Colors to edit color schemes — background, text, button, and accent — then assign a scheme to any section. Under Theme settings → Typography you can pick any heading and body font from Shopify's font library; Botanica pairs Fraunces (headings) with Inter (body) by default.

How do I show or hide badges on product cards?

Go to Theme settings → Badges. You can independently toggle the sale, sold out, care level, and light requirement badges that appear on product cards across your store.

How do I get support?

Head to our support page and send us a message with your store URL and a description of the issue — screenshots help a lot. We answer bug reports and theme setup questions within two business days.
