Botanica

A flexible, performance-focused Shopify OS 2.0 theme for plant shops, home & decor brands, and wellness stores.

Version 3.0.0

Botanica is a modern Online Store 2.0 theme built for speed, accessibility, and easy customization — no code required. It ships with three ready-made industry presets, so you can start from a look that already fits your brand and make it your own in the theme editor.

Botanica

The signature preset. Botanical greens, warm cream backgrounds, and editorial typography for plant shops and garden brands.

Home & Decor

A warm, refined look tuned for furniture, ceramics, and interior goods, with layouts that let large product photography breathe.

Wellness

A calm, minimal starting point for skincare, supplements, and self-care brands, with soft colors and generous spacing.

Installation

Install Botanica by uploading the theme ZIP file in your Shopify admin:

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes.
  2. In the Theme library section, click Add theme → Upload zip file.
  3. Select the Botanica ZIP file and wait for the upload to finish. The theme appears in your theme library — your live store is not affected yet.
  4. Click Customize to open the theme editor and start setting up your store, or Actions → Publish when you're ready to go live.

Switching presets

Botanica includes three industry presets: Botanica, Home & Decor, and Wellness. Each preset is a complete set of theme settings — colors, fonts, and layout defaults — designed for that industry.

To switch presets, open the theme editor (Online Store → Themes → Customize), click the theme name at the top of the editor sidebar, and choose the preset you want. If you add the theme from the Shopify Theme Store, you can also pick a preset when you add it to your library.

Tip: Switching a preset replaces your current theme settings with that preset's defaults. Before switching, duplicate your theme (Actions → Duplicate) so you can roll back if you change your mind.

Quick start

After installing, these four steps get your store ready for visitors:

  1. Set your logo and favicon. In the theme editor, open Theme settings → Logo to upload your logo and favicon, and adjust the logo width.
  2. Configure your header menu. Create your navigation under Online Store → Navigation, then assign it in the Header section. The header supports mega-menu blocks for collection lists, promo cards, and custom links.
  3. Pick a preset. Choose the industry preset closest to your brand (see Switching presets above), then fine-tune colors and fonts under Theme settings.
  4. Add collections and products. Create collections under Products → Collections, add your products, then use the Featured collection and Collection list sections to merchandise your homepage.
