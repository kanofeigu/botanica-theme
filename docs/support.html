Support

Questions about setting up Botanica, or think you've found a bug? Send us a message below — we read every one.

Support policy

  • Bug fixes. If something in the theme doesn't work as documented, we'll fix it and ship an update.
  • Theme setup questions. Not sure which setting controls what, or how to achieve a look from our demos? Ask away — we're glad to point you in the right direction.
  • Fast replies. We respond within two business days (Monday–Friday).
  • Not included: custom code changes, design customization, and support for third-party apps fall outside theme support. For larger projects we recommend working with a Shopify Expert.

Contact us

Fill in the form and we'll get back to you by email. The more detail you include — and a screenshot or two — the faster we can help.
